About Crown & Glory

Where every client leaves feeling like royalty

Our History

Founded in 2024, Crown & Glory Hair Studio started as an independent hair styling venture committed to healthy hair care and modern trends. What began with a few friends in a student residence has grown into a studio loved across Johannesburg.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To provide high-quality, long-lasting hair transformations while maintaining total hair health.

Vision: To become a top-rated local studio recognised for creativity, client care and top-tier styling.

Meet the Lead Stylist

Our lead stylist has years of experience in braiding, weaving and wig maintenance, bringing passion and detail to every hairstyle.
